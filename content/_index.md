Mr. Tu（兔）, writing from Shanghai.

Go pays the bills. Curiosity goes into the rest: keeping a human hand on the wheel of AI/SI agents, teaching voice models not to wait for silence before speaking, and slowly learning Rust for the Linux kernel. My first piece along that road is [rust-null](https://github.com/ubeep/rust-null)—a userspace `/dev/null`, waiting to become a real device.

When I write in English, it is usually about software: how a twenty-line script quietly turns into a weekend-long rabbit hole. When I switch to Chinese, the keyboard gives way to a camera—rain falling on Nanxun, the Master of the Nets Garden after closing time,  a church in Jiaxing…

You can find the source code on [GitHub](https://github.com/ubeep), or learn more at [opentu.tech](https://note.pp.ua).
