Duet, writing from Shanghai.

Go pays the bills. Curiosity goes into the rest: keeping a human hand on the wheel of AI agents, teaching voice models not to wait for silence before speaking, and slowly learning Rust for the Linux kernel. My first piece along that road is [rust-null](https://github.com/ubeep/rust-null), a userspace `/dev/null`, waiting to become a real device.

When I write in English, it is usually about software: how a twenty-line script quietly turns into a weekend-long rabbit hole. When I switch to Chinese, the keyboard gives way to a camera. Rain on Nanxun, the Master of the Nets Garden after closing time, a church in Jiaxing.

The source is on [GitHub](https://github.com/ubeep). I am on [X](https://x.com/aiduet).
